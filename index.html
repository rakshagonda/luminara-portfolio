A LITTLE KINGDOM BEYOND THE MOON

Somewhere,
magic remembers.

Beyond the moonlight lies a kingdom that has forgotten how to shine. Its memories are fading, and a little dreamkeeper needs your help to find the lost magic.

A little adventure is waiting for you

✧ follow the light ✧

CHAPTER I · THE BEGINNING

The river is forgetting...

Once upon a time, Luminara glowed with the memories of its people. Every laugh became a star, every promise became a light, and every cherished moment flowed through a magical river.

But one night, the stars began to disappear. The river grew dim, and the people slowly forgot the things they loved most.

There was only one little girl who could still remember. A girl in a blue dress, carrying a secret in her tiny doll-shaped hair clip.

CHAPTER II · THE DREAMKEEPER

Meet the girl who remembers.

Gentle, curious, and a little mysterious, Luminara wanders through forgotten places, collecting the memories that everyone else has left behind.

HER LITTLE CHARACTER SHEET

Who is Luminara?

Name: Luminara, the Dreamkeeper

Species: Human girl

Appearance: Soft black hair, curious eyes, a blue dress, and a tiny doll-shaped hair clip.

Personality: Gentle, curious, imaginative, and determined to keep her promises.

Talent: Finding forgotten memories and restoring their magic.

Origin: She lives in a kingdom where memories are fading from a magical river.

Dream: To bring every forgotten memory home.

Tagline: “Even forgotten things deserve to be remembered.”

“I promised I would never forget you.”

But who was she talking to?

You found the hidden memory!

The little doll was Luminara's very first friend. Even when the stars went dark, she remembered the promise she had made. A tiny light flickered deep inside the forgotten library.

CHAPTER III · THE FORGOTTEN LIBRARY

Every memory holds a little magic.

Beneath an abandoned tower lies a library filled with dolls. Each one holds a memory someone, somewhere, has forgotten.

CHAPTER IV · THE LAST MEMORY

She finally remembered.

Luminara looked at the doll-shaped clip in her hair. Suddenly, she remembered the lonely doll she had found long ago in the forgotten library.

“I promised I would never forget you,” she whispered. “Even if the whole world does.”

And in that moment, the river began to glow. One by one, the forgotten memories returned. Luminara remembered how to shine.
